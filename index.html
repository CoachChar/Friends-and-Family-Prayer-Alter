How It Works

Come as you are. Leave knowing someone is praying.

No complicated process. No login required. Just a simple, loving way to share a need, stand in agreement, and encourage one another through prayer.

🙏

Share a Prayer Need

Submit a request for yourself, a friend, or a family member. You can choose how much information is shared.

♡

Stand in Agreement

Visit the prayer wall and lift someone before God. A simple “I Prayed” lets them know they are not standing alone.

✦

Return With a Testimony

Come back with updates and answered prayers so our family can celebrate what God is doing together.
